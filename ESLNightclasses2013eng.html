Information about Evening English classes for adults Manor High School

 

Welcome to English classes at Manor ISD!  Many students like you have attended our classes and have been successful toward meeting their goals.  Classes will be offered for Beginning and Intermediate ESL students only.

Due to the large number of students interested in English classes, class registration/orientation will be by individual appointment only.

  1. Interested students may call 512-229-5183 and leave a message.  Please leave at least 2 phone numbers where we may reach you!

 2. You will receive a callback with a time and date to come in for registration/orientation.

 

 

*****All classes will be held on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 6-8PM*****

*****Classes will be held at Manor Excel Academy – NEW LOCATION*****

*****Child care/Homework Hall MAY BE available on a very limited basis for children ages 5-10.*****

NEW students and returning students in good standing from 2013-2014 are welcome to call and leave a message.  Please leave only one message - we will return calls beginning in late August. 

Classes begin in early September 2014 and end in late May 2015.  Classes will follow the Manor ISD calendar. 

In order to be successful, it is very important that you agree to attend classes regularly.  Students are required to attend 60 or more hours of classes during the school year. If you have to miss classes or your schedule changes, please let your teacher know.  If you cannot attend at least 75 percent of class time each month, you will be put on a waiting list to return to class.

Thank you for your interest in English classes for adults
