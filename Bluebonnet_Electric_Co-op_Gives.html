Bluebonnet Electric Co-op Gives Advocacy Outreach $20,000

Bluebonnet Electric Co-op, through a competitive application process, selected 38 organizations within their service area to support with community grants. Advocacy Outreach was one of those selected and representatives from Bluebonnet Electric Co-op paid a visit to the offices on Thursday, December 10, and gave Executive Director, Beth Rolingson, a check for $20,000. The money will be used to support the Early Childhood Development Center, a vital component of the Family Literacy Program.

Thank you, Bluebonnet Electric Co-op!!

Pictured: Deborah Rogers (Bluebonnet), Suanne Tumlinson (Bluebonnet), Beth Rolingson (Executive Director, Advocacy Outreach), Patsy Hoffman (Office Manager, Advocacy Outreach), Ruby Simms (Board of Directors, Advocacy Outreach), Johnny Sanders (Bluebonnet Electric)
