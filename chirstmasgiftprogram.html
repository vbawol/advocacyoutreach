Our Partners

 

Advocacy Outreach Christmas Gifts Program

Many individuals and businesses have donated to make many community families enjoy the Christmas season, no matter what their religious practices are.

For some of us, Christmas was a time of excitement, magic, and joy.  For parents in families who are struggling, financially, Christmas can be a time of worry and, for their children, disappointment. 

Our Donors contribute to the families in need to provide Christmas gifts to children, creating hope and joy!

Individuals and families from Elgin, Bastrop, Manor, Austin, Pfugerville, Round Rock and nation wide have contributes to help create a joyous family experience as well as civic organizations, faith-based groups, and school groups.

Advocacy Outreach manages an application process, interviews applicants and distributes the generous donations that are given each year! 

Who is eligible?

Families with low income.

What documentation is needed?

Proof of income & proof of identity.

How does a family apply?

Advocacy Outreach will post an announcement in its offices of the date when applications will be accepted. Our staff will assist applicants to fill out the application form, if needed.

How does a donor join the program?

People or groups interested in donating to create a joyful Christmas experience for those who would otherwise do without please call Patsy Hoffman at (512) 281-4180.

Our Christmas program is only successful because of the generous donors! Your donations are more than a gift; they create joy and fill a desperate need! Thank you to all our past Donors!
