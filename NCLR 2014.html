Mobilize to Vote 2014

Today, National Council of La Raza partnered with Mi Familia Vota to announce the launch of our “Mobilize to Vote 2014”—a voter registration campaign to register 250,000 Latinos! Our goal is simple: give Latinos the tools necessary to amplify their voices in upcoming midterm elections, and beyond.

With over 8.6 million eligible Latino voters who are not currently registered, a midterm voter registration campaign like this could have major implications in many state and local elections this November, and go a long way toward furthering our community’s priorities—priorities shared by all Americans.

Across the country, Latinos are paying attention to what candidates say and do, and through our “Mobilize to Vote 2014” efforts we will ensure that Latinos are prepared to have their voices heard on Election Day.
