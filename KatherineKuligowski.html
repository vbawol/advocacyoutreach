The staff of Advocacy Outreach wishes to express our deepest sorrow on the passing of Katherine Shirley Crowley Kuligowski on January 3, 2014.

Katherine donated $500. to our annual Christmas giving program the last two years. She is remembered by her family and friends as a loving and compassionate lady who modeled acceptance, thought of others constantly, and shared her joy through storytelling and listening.
We will miss her!
